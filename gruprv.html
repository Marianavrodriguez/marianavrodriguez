GrupRV

Project hero image — new logo, large and centered, on deep navy

From Noise To Authority

Grup RV had grown over 50 years from a single company, Rosa dels Vents, into a multi-sector holding — hotels, weddings, experiences, B2B services — but its identity hadn't kept pace. Up to seven versions of the same logo were in circulation, each still carrying a "Grup Rosa dels Vents" descriptor that added noise, not authority. I audited every version in use, then designed a single mark that doesn't need a subtitle to explain itself — freeing every brand underneath it to lead its own market, with GrupRV as the seal of trust behind them.
