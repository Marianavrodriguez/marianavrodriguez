mod

Creating a new specialty coffee brand from scratch

Urbany Hostels wanted to incorporate a specialty coffee corner in reception. The problem? The only brief was "we need a name and a logo", so here is where my research of the specialty coffee market began. I needed to define a strategy based on the only thing I knew about the brand: it’d be to-go only and it’d be located next to the reception desk. It was during research that I found out the real challenge, Barcelona is one of the cities with the highest concentration of specialty coffee shops. From there, I knew this brand had to be different, it needed to differentiate itself from the idea that good coffee is a luxury, not accessible.
mod was built with the vision of a world where specialty coffee is everyone’s starting point.

I worked through dozens of naming directions before landing on mod. Mod is a nod to the gaming idea of a small addition that upgrades the whole experience, much like adding a specialty coffee corner to a youth hostel or a cup of good coffee to your routine.
I paired it with Martian Mono, a monospace coding typeface, and built the identity around greens and a retro-coding personality that makes the brand distinct.
