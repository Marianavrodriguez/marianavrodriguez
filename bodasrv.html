Bodas RV

Unifying A Broken Brand

RVHotels' wedding business ran across five venues, each with its own website, Instagram and visual language, making it harder to scale marketing or deliver a consistent experience. Together with the team, we defined the strategy: stop competing with each other and start building one strong brand.

Branding Graphic Design

Logo Suite • Typography System • Color Palette

Custom Iconography • Print & Digital Collateral

Brand Manual • Implementation Support

"Before" — the chaos of 3 separate venue brands prior to unification

Bodas RV became the single identity that tied all five venues together. At its core, I built a color and illustration system designed so each venue could express its own personality while remaining unmistakably part of the same brand. A full logo suite, typography and color system, custom iconography, and print and digital collateral, documented in a brand manual so every venue could apply it consistently.

+46% web traffic in the first year after launch.
